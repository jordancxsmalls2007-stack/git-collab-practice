One evening, a college student discovered a mysterious USB drive outside the library. When he plugged it into his computer, a strange message appeared on the screen. The message warned him that he had only 24 hours to solve a mystery.
Curious about the mysterious message, he searched the USB drive and discovered a hidden folder containing encrypted files. As he tried to unlock them, his computer suddenly displayed a countdown timer.
After several attempts, he finally cracked the encryption and discovered that the files contained clues to a hidden time capsule beneath the university. He followed the clues and found the capsule just before the countdown reached zero. Inside was a letter revealing that the entire mystery had been created by a former student to challenge future generations.
